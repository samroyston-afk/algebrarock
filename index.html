        About the game

        For grown-ups

        What it practises

        Nine nights of algebra, in roughly the order schools in England teach it. The school year chosen in band settings opens the matching nights. The rest unlock as your child earns picks.

          How it works

          • Gigs are timed sets. Right answers win fans, and a run of right answers turns up the volume for bonus fans.
          • Soundcheck has no clock. It offers a tip, and after two wrong tries it shows the method step by step.
          • After each gig, wrong answers are listed with the worked method.

          Privacy policy

          Rock the Unknown is made by its developer. This policy was last updated on 5 November 2026.

          We collect nothing. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases. It asks for no permissions and cannot connect to the internet.

          What stays on this device. The band name, instrument, school year, fans, picks and best scores are saved in the app's private storage on this device so the game remembers progress. Other apps cannot read it, it is left out of cloud backups, and it is never sent to us or anyone else.

          Deleting it. Use "Start the tour again" in band settings, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app.

          Children. The game is made for children. Because it collects no personal information, there is nothing to share with anyone.

          Changes. If this ever changes, we will update this policy before releasing the new version.

          Contact. See the app's Google Play listing.
